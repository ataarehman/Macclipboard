Privacy

Your clipboard history stays on this Mac

MacClipboard is a local utility. It does not create accounts, use cloud storage, send analytics, or upload clipboard contents.

History is stored at ~/Library/Application Support/MacClipboard.

This site on macclipboard.mernify.co has no analytics or advertising trackers.

Use excluded apps, pause monitoring, and clear history for sensitive copies. Transient and concealed pasteboard markers are skipped when other apps set them.
